MixMind Sealed Cue Test — Phase 7

This retires the failed full-song runner and adds a short bounded cue test.
After sealing a candidate, a “Sealed Lite Cue Test” panel appears. Start begins
8 seconds before the sealed outgoing boundary; the panel shows elapsed time,
phase, seconds until takeover, source times, and source count.

Controls: Start/Restart and Stop only. Pause/Seek are intentionally unavailable.
Do not use the normal player controls during this isolated test.

Install, Ctrl+Shift+R, Scan Lite Pairs, Seal Lite Test Plan, then click Start /
Restart (8s cue) in the cue panel. Test Stop once in pre-roll and once during
underlay before judging audio quality.
