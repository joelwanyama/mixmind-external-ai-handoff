Short Duration Candidate Ladder — Phase 13

The inspector showed that a zero-drift pair was being tested only at 6.00s.
This update retains the strict vocal/interval gates but evaluates a duration
ladder from the feasible maximum down to 1.50s in 0.25s steps.

It does not lower thresholds. It asks whether a shorter fully-proven Lite
handoff exists when a long 6-second entry fails.

After installation, Scan Lite Pairs -> Inspect Evidence. The panel will show
multiple candidate durations (for example 6.00s, 5.75s ... 1.50s).
