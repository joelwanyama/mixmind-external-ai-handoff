Cue Emergency Stop Fix

The cue Stop control failed. This update makes Stop an explicit emergency
teardown rather than relying only on the ledger:
- increments the session token, invalidating future scheduling;
- calls stop(current AudioContext time) on every cue source;
- cancels gain automation and sets gain to zero;
- disconnects source, gain, and filter chains;
- clears the ledger;
- shows a Stops count in the cue panel.

Install, seal the matching plan, start the cue, press Stop during PRE-ROLL, and
send a screenshot showing State STOPPED, Sources 0/3, and Stops incremented.
Wait 15 seconds to confirm no scheduled incoming audio appears.
